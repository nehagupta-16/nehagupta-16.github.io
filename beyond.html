I've never quite trusted the version of a person you get from their publication list. Here is the rest of it — the things I do when the pipelines are running, and the things that have shaped how I work more than any course did.

Badminton

The sport I actually care about. It's the only hour of my week where the feedback is instant and nobody wants to discuss methodology — you either got to the shuttle or you didn't. I'm competitive about it in a way that reliably surprises people who have only met me at a whiteboard.

Baking

Chemistry with a better output. There's something restful about a protocol where following the steps exactly is the whole point, rather than the beginning of an argument about parameters. Precise ratios, controlled temperature, reproducible results — and at the end, cake.

Guitar

Self-taught, still bad, having a very good time. I'd recommend picking up something you're genuinely a beginner at to anyone a few years into a PhD — it's a useful reminder of what it feels like to not know things yet, which is easy to forget once you're the person other people ask.

Community Indian Student Association, UGA · 2024–25

I served as President of UGA's Indian Student Association. In practice this meant budgets, volunteers, venue bookings and an enormous amount of food — but underneath that it meant giving people who had just moved across the world somewhere to land. I was named Student Leader of the Year by International Student Life in 2025, which I'm still quietly pleased about.

Mentoring

I mentored an NSF REU student through a full summer research project in 2025. Watching someone go from "what is a FASTQ file" to presenting their own results in ten weeks is, honestly, the most rewarding thing I've done in graduate school.

Moving Noida → Boston → Athens

Three cities in ten years, each of them a full restart — new country, new weather, new everything. I've gotten good at arriving somewhere with no network and slowly building one. It's the part of my life that shows up most in how I work: I'm not thrown by starting from scratch.
