Most of what I do starts with the same problem: a bulk experiment averages away exactly the cells you care about. Single-cell and single-nucleus sequencing gives that resolution back, but it also hands you a dataset that is noisy, sparse and easy to over-interpret. A good deal of my work is simply the discipline of getting from those reads to a claim you'd be willing to defend.

The other half is structural. Once you know a kinase is behaving strangely in a particular cell type, the natural next question is why — and that's a question about the protein itself. Being able to move between the transcript and the structure is the part of this work I find most satisfying.

What I work on

Single-cell & isoform transcriptomics UGA · 2023 – present

Resolving cell-type-specific kinase expression and splicing in neuronal cells, down to individual isoforms. I'm also applying a single-cell co-expression framework to identify kinase–substrate networks that are altered in Alzheimer's disease (manuscript in preparation).

Kinase structure & function UGA · AlphaFold3 · PyMOL

Characterising kinase regulatory motifs and catalytic features through structural modeling. This contributed to a 2025 Journal of Biological Chemistry study of DCLK3, an understudied kinase linked to neurodegeneration, and how it regulates itself and chooses its substrates.

LLM agents for kinase biology UGA · manuscript in preparation

Contributing to a multi-agent LLM system that predicts cancer mutations affecting phosphorylation sites and generates testable hypotheses about kinase–substrate specificity.

Neuroimmunology & Alzheimer's Broad Institute · 2021

Analysed single-cell and single-nucleus RNA-seq from TIM-3 (Havcr2)-deficient 5×FAD mice — preprocessing, QC, integration, clustering, differential expression and annotation — helping define a microglial subpopulation with increased pro-phagocytic and reduced pro-inflammatory gene expression (Nature, 2025). I also supported a multi-omics study of the lipid kinase PI5P4Kγ, integrating transcriptomic and chromatin data.

Pipelines & data quality UGA · Cellarity · Eminent Biosciences

I like building infrastructure other people depend on. For UGA's Computational Biology Consulting Group I built reproducible scRNA-seq and whole-genome variant-calling pipelines on a SLURM-managed cluster used by campus labs. At Cellarity, in a drug-discovery setting, I analysed single-cell and Illumina data, designed QC modules that caught low-quality samples and runs before downstream analysis, and maintained processing pipelines alongside wet-lab scientists. My first pipeline, at Eminent Biosciences, used HISAT2, StringTie and DESeq2 on paired normal and colon cancer samples.

Drug discovery & docking Where I started · Molecules, 2021

My first-author paper modelled steroidal alkaloids from Holarrhena pubescens against guanylyl cyclase C using docking and molecular dynamics. It still shapes how I think about a target: is an idea even chemically plausible before anyone spends a year on it?
