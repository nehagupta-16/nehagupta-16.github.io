Most of what I do starts with the same problem: a bulk experiment averages away exactly the cells you care about. Single-cell and single-nucleus sequencing gives that resolution back, but it also hands you a dataset that is noisy, sparse and easy to over-interpret. A good deal of my work is simply the discipline of getting from those reads to a claim you'd be willing to defend.

The other half is structural. Once you know a kinase is behaving strangely in a particular cell type, the natural next question is why — and that's a question about the protein itself. Being able to move between the transcript and the structure is the part of this work I find most satisfying.

What I work on

Single-cell & isoform transcriptomics scRNA-seq · snRNA-seq · spatial

Resolving cell-type-specific kinase expression and splicing in neuronal cells, at the level of individual isoforms. I'm also applying a single-cell co-expression framework to identify kinase–substrate networks that are altered in Alzheimer's disease (manuscript in preparation).

Kinase structure & function AlphaFold3 · PyMOL

Characterising regulatory motifs and catalytic features through structural modeling. This fed into a 2025 Journal of Biological Chemistry study of DCLK3 — an understudied kinase linked to neurodegeneration — and how it regulates itself and chooses its substrates.

LLM agents for kinase biology Multi-agent systems

I'm contributing to a multi-agent LLM system that predicts cancer mutations affecting phosphorylation sites and generates testable hypotheses about kinase–substrate specificity (manuscript in preparation).

Reproducible pipelines SLURM · HPC · GATK · STAR

For UGA's Computational Biology Consulting Group I've built reproducible scRNA-seq and whole-genome variant-calling pipelines on a SLURM-managed cluster that campus research labs use for their analyses. Before that, at Cellarity, I designed the QC modules that caught low-quality samples and runs before they reached downstream analysis.

Neurodegeneration & neuroimmunology The through-line

At the Broad Institute I analysed single-cell and single-nucleus data from TIM-3 (Havcr2)-deficient 5×FAD Alzheimer's mouse models, helping define a microglial subpopulation with increased pro-phagocytic and reduced pro-inflammatory gene expression — part of a 2025 Nature paper.

Drug discovery & docking Where I started

My first-author paper modelled steroidal alkaloids from Holarrhena pubescens against guanylyl cyclase C using docking and molecular dynamics. It still shapes how I think about a target: is an idea even chemically plausible before anyone spends a year on it?
